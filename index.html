About the Research

This repository provides a structured approach to vocabulary acquisition through historical linguistics. By analyzing Germanic sound shifts (Grimm's Law) and Scots cognates, learners can decode advanced English vocabulary via underlying root logic rather than rote memorization.

Designed for academic researchers, international curriculum coordinators, and advanced exam candidates (GRE/SAT/IELTS).
